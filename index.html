Ellie Sidwell

Original seascapes, landscapes and abstract paintings, built up in layers of colour, texture and gesture.

I'm an artist inspired by the beauty of the natural landscape, the changing seasons and the little details that make a place feel special. My work explores colour, texture and atmosphere, creating expressive contemporary interpretations of the British countryside. I love the freedom of loose brushstrokes, building layers using collage, allowing a painting to evolve organically, capturing not just how a landscape looks but how it feels.

I'm drawn to the contrast between light and shade, open skies, winding paths, fields and seascapes. My paintings are often abstract or semi abstract with emphasis on movement, energy and the emotion of a place.

I create original paintings for people who want to bring colour, character and a connection to nature into their homes. I believe art we have in our homes should bring us joy every day.

Commissions

Ellie is available for commissions. If you have a space in mind, a colour palette you love, or a place that means something to you, she would be happy to create a piece for you.

Get in touch with a little about what you are looking for, including rough size and where it will hang.
